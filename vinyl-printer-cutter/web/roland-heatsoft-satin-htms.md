# Roland HeatSoft SATIN ESM-HTMS

Frozen snapshot of Roland DGA product page for HeatSoft™ SATIN (ESM-HTMS).

## Overview

HeatSoft SATIN is printable heat transfer film for digital inkjet workflows. It weeds easily, transfers smoothly, and is described as producing a screen-printed look on cotton, polyester, and poly/cotton.

Compatible with Roland VersaStudio BN-20 Series and other listed Roland eco-solvent printers/cutters.

## Features

- Use in conjunction with PTM2 mask
- 3 mil polyurethane film on polyester liner
- Easy to transfer to mask without a squeegee
- High opacity for use with dark fabrics
- High white point compared to other transfer materials
- Softer hand than most other transfer materials

## Specifications

- Total caliper: 209 microns (film and liner)
- Opacity: 90%
- Inkset: Eco solvent (Eco-Sol MAX, Eco-Sol MAX 2, EJ, TrueVIS)
- Adhesive: Thermally activated
- Backing: Polyester

## Available sizes

- 54" × 25Y
- 30" × 25Y (29.5" actual width)
- 20" × 25Y (19.6" actual width)

## Storage

65°–75°F (18°–24°C), 30–50% RH; acclimate 24–48 hours before use if stored outside that range.
