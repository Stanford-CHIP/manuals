# Stahls CAD-COLOR SuperTEK Matte Clear

Frozen snapshot of Stahls’ product page for CAD-COLOR® SuperTEK® Matte Clear printable heat transfer vinyl.

## Overview

Clear printable PU film for full-color transfers with a matte finish. Thin, stretchy, soft hand; aimed at performance garments.

## Key constraints

- For white or light fabrics only
- Mask is not needed
- For solvent inks on a print/cut system only — not inkjet or laser
- Available in 20" wide rolls

## Material specifications

- Composition: Polyurethane (PU)
- Carrier: Frosted, non-pressure sensitive
- Thickness: 37 microns (0.037 mm)
- Dry time: 30–60 minutes
- Washability: 50 (per page rating)
- Safety: CPSIA certified

## Print / cut / press (summary)

1. Print and cut mirrored on the adhesive side of the roll
2. After dry, weed excess
3. Place weeded design in positive orientation on the garment
4. Example press: tack 5 seconds, peel warm, cover and seal 5 seconds at 280–300°F / 138–150°C, medium pressure (Hotronix #6)

## Cutter guidelines (from page)

Roland GX-24 / VP-300 / SP-300 / XC-540: 45° blade, 70–90 g, offset 0.25, 30 cm/s. Always test cut.
