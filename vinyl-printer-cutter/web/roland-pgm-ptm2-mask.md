# Roland HeatSoft PGM-PTM2 transfer mask

Frozen snapshot of Roland DGA product page for HeatSoft™ PLUS PGM-PTM2 Medium Tack Polyester Transfer Mask.

## Overview

100% polyester transfer tape for printed and cut graphics produced with HeatSoft PLUS ESM-HTM3 and Roland Eco-Sol Max / Eco-Sol MAX 2 inks.

Roles of the mask:

- Lift the graphic from its backing liner without removing ink from the print
- Keep thin stretchy media dimensionally stable during heat transfer
- Keep multi-piece designs aligned
- Act as a protective barrier between the graphic and the heat press

## Specifications

- Total caliper: 4.7 mils
- Adhesive: Water-based natural
- Backing: Paper

## Available sizes

- 54"
- 30" (actual 29.5")
- 20" (actual 19.66")

## Related

Paired with ESM-HTM3 (and commonly used with other HeatSoft printable films such as HTMS per those product pages).
