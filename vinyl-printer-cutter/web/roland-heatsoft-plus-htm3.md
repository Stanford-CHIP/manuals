# Roland HeatSoft PLUS ESM-HTM3

Frozen snapshot of Roland DGA product page for HeatSoft™ PLUS (ESM-HTM3).

## Overview

Printable polyurethane heat transfer material. Stretches and weeds easily. Intended for cotton, polyester, poly-blend, spandex/Lycra, and leather. Soft hand; claimed durability up to 50 wash cycles.

Graphics are lifted from the backing liner with HeatSoft PGM-PTM2 heat transfer mask for application.

Compatible with Roland VersaStudio, VersaCAMM SP-i / VS-i, and TrueVIS printers (per product page).

## Specifications

- Total caliper: 8 mils (film and liner)
- Opacity: 90%
- Inkset: Eco solvent (Eco-Sol MAX, Eco-Sol MAX 2, EJ, TrueVIS)
- Adhesive: Thermally activated
- Backing: Clear polyester

## Available sizes

- 54"
- 30" (actual 29.5")
- 20" (actual 19.66")

## Related

Use with PGM-PTM2 transfer mask.
