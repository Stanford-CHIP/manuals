# Siser TTD masks

Frozen snapshot of Siser North America TTD masking materials page.

## Overview

TTD Easy and TTD High Tack are clear application masks formulated for Siser digital / print-and-cut heat transfer media. Ordinary sign-vinyl application tape is not suitable for HTV: it can leave residue and be hard to remove after heat.

The masks are clear so the design can be placed accurately on the garment, then release after heat application without residue.

## TTD Easy Mask

Medium-tack clear mask for Siser print-and-cut / digital media. Place the image, heat apply, release cleanly.

## TTD High Tack Mask

Higher tack for firmer grip; reduces wrinkling/warping during masking. Recommended for large or detailed designs and materials that need extra grip (e.g. EasyPatterns and other digital media). Warm peel; store masked designs back on the multipurpose paper backing.

## Handling note

In heat/humidity, allow masks to return to indoor room temperature (below 70°F / 21°C) before use; brief fridge/freezer cool-down (5–10 minutes) if needed.
